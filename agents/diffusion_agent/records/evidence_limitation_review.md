# Revision: explicitly diagnose a weak diffusion claim

## Claim under review
“RAG has reached the early-majority diffusion stage across industries and geographies.”

This is a deliberately unsupported challenge claim for review, not a statement asserted by the saved responses.

## Evidence and limitation
The primary and contrast fixtures supply synthetic snapshot S1, E1: 60 of 100 midsize US service organizations in a convenience panel use RAG in production for at least one unspecified application during the prior 12 months ending 2026-10-25.

This is synthetic test evidence, not a real survey or external source. Even accepting its stipulated facts for this evaluation, the sample is geographically narrow, industry-specific and unrepresentative. It provides no adoption sequence, individual relative adoption timing, representative denominator, or application breakdown. A cross-sectional prevalence count alone does not establish Rogers' early-majority stage. Its recent fixture date does not overcome those scope and method limitations. There is no basis to call it stale; the weaknesses here are representativeness, transferability and stage inference.

## Defensible replacement
Within the stipulated panel, 60/100 organizations use RAG in production for some use. That observation does not establish diffusion stage across industries or countries, nor adoption of internal IT knowledge-answer drafting. For that application the separate E2 count is 20 routine deployments, 30 pilots and 50 nonadopters in the same nonrepresentative panel. Broad diffusion stage remains unknown.

## Confidence and management implication
High confidence only in reproducing stipulated counts; no confident real-world or cross-industry stage conclusion. Do not use the broad stage claim to justify a rollout or fear-of-missing-out deadline. Preserve the primary case's safeguarded learning pilot and the contrast case's conditional evidence-based deferral; posture changes evidence preferences and timing, not the strength or scope of S1.

Needed: dated, independently verifiable, methodologically transparent and representative adoption evidence across relevant regions and sectors, longitudinal adoption timing, definitions separating pilots from routine production, and application-specific outcomes from genuinely comparable peers.

## Agent revision and review
The specialist instructions now require an explicit claim-level limitation diagnosis, narrower supported finding, confidence adjustment, evidence request and management implication within the existing JSON schema. A scope-challenge review criterion was added. Manual review confirms this diagnosis preserves the technology/application/peer distinction and does not change diffusion stage based on posture.

The previously committed synthetic responses already withhold a formal population-wide stage and flag the convenience sample. They remain historical evaluation artifacts; this revision does not claim a new independent model run.
