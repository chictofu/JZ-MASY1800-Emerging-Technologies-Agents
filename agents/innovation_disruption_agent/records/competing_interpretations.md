# Competing classification and strongest incumbent counter-case

AI-assisted analytical argument for the hypothetical advertising agency. No actual
customer, market or performance evidence is asserted. This is not a separate ChatGPT run.

## Preferred working classification
The cautious internal recommendation application is prospective incremental process
innovation: it could improve fixed-rule/manual deal selection while retaining human
accountability. The general technology finding remains provisional incremental
innovation at the stipulated prediction/ranking family level, not a verified D1 breakthrough.

## Strongest competing classification: adoption or routine automation
The application may merely adopt an established tool, with no new or significantly
improved process demonstrated. D1 could duplicate existing filtering, optimize an
irrelevant proxy, or add review/integration costs that erase the proposed benefit.
Organizational novelty and operational installation alone do not establish innovation.
For a proposed self-service subscription, a new commercial wrapper may be commercialization
of existing capability rather than breakthrough innovation. Customer benefit and a
significantly improved offering remain to be established.

This alternative is plausible because the cases provide no measured advertiser-outcome
lift, net cost improvement, customer demand or implemented application. It does not
change the fixed general technology facts; it challenges application significance.

## Strongest case for no material business-model disruption
The agency may sell accountable outcomes, strategy, relationships, complex advisory
work and coordination rather than routine ranking alone. AI recommendations could
become a complementary input: traders screen faster, experts handle harder work,
clients retain the managed-service relationship and the agency retains fee value.

For self-service, customers may still require oversight and integrated service.
Potential subscription demand might come from new segments rather than existing clients,
creating incremental revenue without substantial cannibalization. Review, support,
integration and switching costs could prevent substitution. These are hypotheses,
not verified customer behavior.

In the synthetic incumbent boundary case, routine curation represents 60% of fee
revenue but only 15% of contribution margin. The stipulated distinction strengthens
the argument that fee exposure alone does not establish a viability threat. It does
not prove that losing those fees is harmless: shared costs, cash flow, cross-selling,
customer acquisition and adaptation costs remain unknown.

## Evidence that distinguishes the alternatives

| Question | Evidence favoring innovation/displacement | Evidence favoring routine adoption/complementarity |
|---|---|---|
| Does the application significantly improve the incumbent workflow? | Comparable campaign tests show decision-relevant outcome or capability improvements after review/integration costs | No meaningful gain, proxy-only improvement or costs offset benefits |
| Is a breakthrough claim defensible? | Replicated discontinuous capability/outcome change against a named baseline within a stated scope | Improvements remain within established capabilities; no replication/generalization |
| Do customers substitute? | Observed switching from managed services to self-service with reduced agency spend | Customers retain managed services; self-service use is additive or confined to new segments |
| Does the agency retain value capture? | Material fee/margin erosion attributable to substitution after delivery/support costs | Retention, pricing and contribution margins remain viable; validated advisory/upsell gains offset losses |
| Does a competitor create urgency? | Documented effective offers, relevant customer adoption and losses attributable to them | Offers are ineffective, unavailable or not adopted; neither their absence nor harmlessness should be assumed |
| Is the organization threatened with obsolescence? | Critical margin/cash-flow dependence plus switching and costly or blocked adaptation | Profitable complementary capabilities and demonstrated affordable adaptation |

## Evaluation design
Define advertiser objectives, minimum meaningful improvement and guardrails before
examining results. Compare with the same fixed-rule/manual baseline using comparable
inventory, budgets, timing and review conditions. Measure advertiser outcomes,
quality failures, trader effort and full delivery costs; do not substitute ranking
accuracy for customer value. Controlled tests require readiness/adoption oversight.

Use observed retention, customer choices, willingness-to-pay tests, fee and contribution
margin changes, and existing-versus-new customer segmentation to assess substitution.
Do not mistake stated interest, a cheaper offering or revenue exposure for observed
switching or existential impact. Separate a revenue/margin stress scenario from an
estimated probability, and retain uncertainty when causal attribution is weak.

## Bounded conclusion
Neither material disruption nor its absence is established by the synthetic cases.
The no-disruption interpretation is strongest for the cautious internal pilot.
Self-service introduces a credible displacement mechanism, but actual disruption is
Undetermined until customer substitution and economics are evidenced. Preserve
both interpretations and state which observations would change the judgment.
