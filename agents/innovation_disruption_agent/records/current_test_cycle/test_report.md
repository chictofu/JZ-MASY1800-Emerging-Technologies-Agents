## What changed and what remained stable
General technology classification stayed stable across all four outputs. Internal recommendations improve a workflow; self-service changes delivery channel, proposed commercialization and potential agency intermediation/fee exposure. Posture changes immediate exposure and action, not technology facts. Primary versus contrast 1 changes both application and posture, so it is not a controlled estimate of either effect. Contrast 1 versus contrast 2 isolates posture within the stated inputs.

## Diagnosed weaknesses and revision
Pre-revision self-service evaluations assigned High conditional impact without establishing customer switching or affected revenue/margin scale. They also described budget execution as an added capability without explicitly recognizing that D1 only scores/ranks. These outputs are retained as failures of evidence calibration/capability scope, despite passing schema checks.

Revision: require one supported rating, use Undetermined when substitution scale is unestablished, place potential High impact in a separate conditional scenario, and state that autonomous execution needs a proposed controller/integration/permissions/controls outside D1's evidenced scope. Final reevaluations apply these changes and maintain a stable general finding.

## Validation and limits
Four inputs pass the full common input schema. Eight before/after responses pass the supplied validator and full common output schema. All four final general findings match. Frozen core verification passes. Same-assistant analytical review confirms explicit levels, organizational baselines, both sides of disruption, alternatives, timing-risk uncertainty and handoffs. This is self-critique and targeted reevaluation, not independent repeated model sessions, a measured optimization experiment, or separate ChatGPT executions. Ratings are scenario reasoning, not empirical business forecasts.

The comparative timing-risk judgment is Undetermined: early-adoption harms and non-response harms are plausible, but no performance, economics or actual competitor adoption establishes their relative magnitude.
