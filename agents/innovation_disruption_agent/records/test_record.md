## What the contrasts demonstrate
The outputs keep exactly the same general ET finding while differentiating intended use and organizational consequences. Primary versus contrast_1 changes application and posture together, so it cannot isolate their causal effects. Contrast_1 versus contrast_2 holds the application and business model fixed and changes posture only; it is the cleaner posture comparison. These are reasoning demonstrations, not empirical estimates of adoption outcomes.

The strongest counter-case is that accountable expertise, client relationships and bundled strategy preserve fees and make self-service complementary. Customer substitution, willingness to pay, affected margin share and adaptation costs would distinguish that account from material displacement.

## Weakness, diagnosis, revision
The v0.1 instruction review found a forced ordinal disruption rating despite missing business-model inputs and insufficient gates for existential claims. v0.2 allows Undetermined, requires viability evidence for existential claims, and separates structural exposure from near-term posture. The final ambiguous output abstains as intended. This is a design-review revision followed by final evaluations; it is not a claimed failed ChatGPT run or a controlled before/after performance experiment.

Original v0.1 prompt is an exploratory generative-AI scenario drafted before the user chose programmatic deal optimization. It is retained only as instruction-review history, not as a required final contrast result.
