# Lab 5 learning → agent behavior

Source: user-uploaded Lab 5.docx, read directly; input hash retained in run_provenance.json.

1. Investment/publicity reflects expectations rather than demonstrated transformation → prohibit hype-only classification; request capability/breadth/depth evidence.
2. Long-term potential does not imply fit today → separate general, application and organizational findings.
3. Investment and adoption might help realize potential → distinguish causal enabling mechanisms from evidence of actual durable effects; neither funding nor adoption counts proves confirmation.
4. When to stop a failed implementation? → define outcome/safety/cost/learning/time gates; stop or redesign local work without abandoning broad monitoring. Thresholds and approval belong to accountable decision-makers.

Refinement: widespread adoption and long historical duration may support realized transformation, but cannot be strict prerequisites for every qualified *potentially* Promethean judgment. The agent requires credible pathways and explicit uncertainty, not proof of the future. Societal significance is also distinct from desirability.

Lab 5 references to AI and the metaverse remain educational examples, not externally verified facts or evidence entries about actual deployment, investment or impact.
